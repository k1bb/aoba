# AOBA Model Architecture and Literature-Grounded Formulation

> **Language**: English | [日本語 (architecture_ja.md)](architecture_ja.md)

## 1. Scientific Objective and Documentation Standard

AOBA investigates two fundamental questions:
1. Given **only** external physical boundary conditions—land–sea/ice-sheet geometry and surface elevation from `Earth2014` (Hirt & Rexer, 2015), planetary rotation rate $\Omega$, solar orbital insolation $S_0$, and well-mixed atmospheric gas concentrations ($\mathrm{CO}_2, \mathrm{O}_2$)—can a coupled atmosphere–surface ocean–terrestrial ecosystem autonomously reproduce Earth's climate and biome zones (benchmarked against Beck et al., 2018, 2023)?
2. How does such a minimally constrained model—free of cumulus parameterizations, prescribed ocean $Q$-fluxes, external soil/albedo maps, and plant-functional-type (PFT) parameter tables—predict future shifts in global climate zones under elevated $\mathrm{CO}_2$?

**Documentation & Traceability Rule**: Following the mathematical completeness of *Description of MIROC6 AGCM* (2021), *Description of MATSIRO6* (2021), and *Detailed formulation of SCALE-RM* (2025), every continuous equation, coordinate transformation, staggered stencil, and constant implemented in `src/aoba/` must be documented explicitly and linked to its supporting source publication (author, year, section, equation number).

## 2. Unified Nonhydrostatic Dynamical Framework, Turbulence, and Orographic Drag

### 2.1 Global Host Core: Spherical Centroidal Voronoi C-Grid (TRiSK) + Charney–Phillips HEVI
- **Primary References**: Ringler, Ju, & Gunzburger (2008, *Ocean Dynamics*, 58, 475–498); Thuburn, Ringler, Skamarock, & Klemp (2009, *J. Comput. Phys.*, 228, 8321–8335); Ringler, Thuburn, Klemp, & Skamarock (2010, *J. Comput. Phys.*, 229, 3065–3090); Klemp (2011, *Mon. Wea. Rev.*, 139, 2163–2169); Staniforth & Thuburn (2012, *Q. J. R. Meteorol. Soc.*, 138, 1–26); Skamarock et al. (2012, *Mon. Wea. Rev.*, 140, 3090–3105); Gassmann (2013, *Q. J. R. Meteorol. Soc.*, 139, 152–175); Held & Suarez (1994, *Bull. Amer. Meteor. Soc.*, 75, 1825–1830); Nishizawa, Tomita, & Team SCALE (2025, *SCALE-RM v5.5.5*, Ch. 2).
- **Governing Equations**: Fully compressible nonhydrostatic flux-form Euler equations conserving dry-air density $\rho_d$, edge-normal horizontal momentum, vertical momentum $\rho_d w$, moist potential temperature $\rho_d \theta_m$ (including vapor-buoyancy virtual effects and consistent compressible diabatic/precipitation enthalpy terms; Seidel & Yang, 2024; Nishizawa et al., 2025, Sec. 2.5–2.7), and moisture scalars $(\rho_d q_n, \rho_d q_p)$.
- **Horizontal SCVT Orthogonal C-Grid**: Generated from recursive icosahedral subdivision and spherical Lloyd iterations (Ringler et al., 2008):
  - `Glevel-4` (standard): $N_{\mathrm{cells}} = 2,562$ cells ($\Delta x \approx 240\ \mathrm{km}$), $N_{\mathrm{edges}} = 7,680$.
  - `Glevel-5` (high-resolution): $N_{\mathrm{cells}} = 10,242$ cells ($\Delta x \approx 120\ \mathrm{km}$), $N_{\mathrm{edges}} = 30,720$.
  - Strictly orthogonal primal Voronoi edges and dual Delaunay segments eliminate A-grid $2\Delta x$ pressure/divergence decoupling in $w$ and Cubed-Sphere non-orthogonal corner imprinting. **TRiSK** tangential velocity reconstruction maintains exact stationary geostrophic modes, energy conservation, and potential-vorticity compatibility, supplemented by horizontal Smagorinsky viscosity and $\nabla^4$ hyperdiffusion (Skamarock et al., 2012; Gassmann, 2013).
- **Vertical Charney–Phillips Grid & Smoothed Terrain-Following Coordinate**: $N_z = 32$ levels up to $z_{\mathrm{top}} \approx 24\ \mathrm{km}$ ($p_{\mathrm{top}} \approx 25\ \mathrm{hPa}$): 13 boundary-layer levels below $2\ \mathrm{km}$ ($\Delta z_1 = 60\ \mathrm{m}$), 14 free-tropospheric levels ($2\text{--}17.5\ \mathrm{km}$), and 5 coarse lower-stratospheric Rayleigh sponge levels ($17.5\text{--}24\ \mathrm{km}$). Co-locating $\theta_m$ and $w$ at layer interfaces (**Charney–Phillips** staggering; Gassmann, 2013) over progressive height-smoothed terrain surfaces (Klemp, 2011) eliminates vertical $2\Delta z$ computational buoyancy modes and spurious steep-mountain updrafts.
- **Subgrid Orographic Blocking & Gravity-Wave Drag**: Subgrid elevation standard deviation $\sigma_{\mathrm{topo}}$, anisotropy, and slope statistics computed directly within each Voronoi cell from 5-arc-min `Earth2014` drive low-level blocked-flow drag and tropospheric gravity-wave momentum deposition (Lott & Miller, 1997, *Q. J. R. Meteorol. Soc.*, 123, 101–127; MIROC6 AGCM Document Writing Team, 2021, Sec. 3.9).
- **HEVI Time Integration**: Three-stage Runge–Kutta (RK3) with Horizontally Explicit Vertically Implicit (HEVI) acoustic/gravity treatment solved via fused batched Thomas tridiagonal kernels and positive-definite Zalesak Flux-Corrected Transport (FCT) scalar advection (Skamarock et al., 2012; Nishizawa et al., 2025, App. A.2).

### 2.2 Displacement-Following Aerodynamic Datum and Modern Roughness Sublayer (RSL)
- **Primary References**: Hirt & Rexer (2015); Kempes et al. (2011); Raupach (1994); Harman & Finnigan (2007); De Ridder (2010, *Boundary-Layer Meteorol.*, 134, 257–267); Bonan et al. (2018, *GMD*, 11, 1467–1496); Lee et al. (2020, *GMD*, 13, 521–536); Abolafia-Rosenzweig et al. (2021, *JAMES*, 13, e2021MS002665); Meier et al. (2022, *GMD*, 15, 2365–2393); Miller, Beljaars, & Palmer (1992, *J. Climate*, 5, 418–434).
- **Boundary Relief & Algebraic Surface Masks**: `Earth2014` (`SUR2014`, `TBI2014`, `ICE2014`) defines cell-averaged elevation $z_{\mathrm{topo}}$, subgrid standard deviation $\sigma_{\mathrm{topo}}$, and exact masks for open water ($\mathrm{SUR} - \mathrm{TBI} > 0$), grounded ice sheets ($\mathrm{ICE} > 0,\ \mathrm{SUR} = \mathrm{TBI}$), and non-glaciated land including below-sea-level endorheic basins.
- **Effective Aerodynamic Datum & RSL Similarity**: Potential canopy height $h_c$ emerges from vascular allometry and local water–energy budgets (Kempes et al., 2011), adjusted for snow burial (MATSIRO6, 2021, Eqs. 2.1–2.3; Abolafia-Rosenzweig et al., 2021), yielding continuous zero-plane displacement $d(h_c, \mathrm{LAI})$ and roughness length $z_{0m}(h_c, \mathrm{LAI})$ (Raupach, 1994) coupled to analytical exponential-integral RSL stability corrections $\hat{\Psi}_{M,H}$ (Harman & Finnigan, 2007; De Ridder, 2010; Bonan et al., 2018; Lee et al., 2020). Anchoring the atmospheric grid base on solid terrain / ice ($z_{w,0} = z_{\mathrm{topo}}$) while applying displacement height to the surface-layer reference height $\tilde{z}_{\mathrm{ref}} = \max(z_{c,0} - z_{w,0} - 0.65 h_c,\ 5.0\ \mathrm{m})$ ensures smooth surface exchange across dense canopies without daily mesh deformation. Over open ocean, roughness combines viscous smooth-flow and Charnock wave terms (Miller et al., 1992).

### 2.3 1-to-1 MAML+ICA Embedded CRM, MYNN Level 2.5 PBL, and 3D SGS Turbulence
- **Primary References**: Khairoutdinov & Randall (2003); Khairoutdinov, Randall, & DeMott (2005); Lee & Khairoutdinov (2015); Lin et al. (2023); Nakanishi & Niino (2009, *JMSJ*, 87, 895–912); Yamazaki & Miura (2024); Nishizawa, Tomita, & Team SCALE (2025, Sec. 8.1.2 & Sec. 8.2.1).
- **Pure 1-to-1 MAML + ICA Superparameterization**: Every host SCVT Voronoi cell embeds one periodic orthogonal C-grid + Charney–Phillips CRM (default `[N_cells, nz = 32, ny = 1, nx = 8]`, $\Delta x = 8\ \mathrm{km}$; TOML-configurable up to 3D `8 × 8` at $4\ \mathrm{km}$) where every subcolumn holds independent 4-band ICA radiation and 1-to-1 MAML land/biosphere/ocean column states, returning 3D convective heating, moistening, and momentum tendencies.
- **Turbulence Closure**: Vertical boundary-layer and stratocumulus-top mixing in every CRM column are governed by implicit **MYNN Level 2.5** TKE closure with buoyancy and liquid-water variance length scales (Nakanishi & Niino, 2009; Nishizawa et al., 2025, Sec. 8.2.1), complemented by anisotropic **Smagorinsky–Lilly** 3D subgrid-scale eddy diffusion (Nishizawa et al., 2025, Sec. 8.1.2).

## 3. Cloud Microphysics and Four-Band Analytical Two-Stream Radiation

### 3.1 Two-Variable Diagnostic-Phase Cloud Microphysics
- **Primary Reference**: Khairoutdinov & Randall (2003, *J. Atmos. Sci.*, 60, App. A–B).
- **Formulation**: Prognostic non-precipitating water $q_n = q_v + q_c + q_i$ and precipitating water $q_p = q_r + q_s$ partition condensate $\max(0, q_n - q^*(T, p))$ and precipitation diagnostically between liquid and ice via $\omega(T) \in [0, 1]$ over $[253.15, 273.16]\ \mathrm{K}$, resolving autoconversion, accretion, sedimentation, evaporation/sublimation, fusion latent heat, and cirrus radiative effects.

### 3.2 Lookup-Table-Free Four-Band Two-Stream Radiation
- **Primary References**: Toon et al. (1989); Lacis & Hansen (1974); Slingo (1989); Ebert & Curry (1992); Weaver & Ramanathan (1995); Koll & Cronin (2018); Wing et al. (2018); Sherwood et al. (2020).
- **Solver Architecture**: Unified tridiagonal two-stream solver (Toon et al., 1989) evaluated every $\Delta t_{\mathrm{rad}} = 900\ \mathrm{s}$ across all columns with instantaneous solar-zenith scaling $\mu_0(t)$, returning direct-beam and diffuse downward shortwave fluxes separately in SW1 (UV + Visible/PAR) and SW2 (NIR) alongside LW1 ($\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{CO}_2$) and LW2 ($8\text{--}12\ \mu\mathrm{m}$ window) longwave fluxes.

## 4. Subgrid Two-Leaf Autonomous Biosphere, Freeze–Thaw Soil, Cryosphere, and Ekman Ocean

Each of the 64 CRM columns per host cell couples 1-to-1 to an underlying surface tile following the SLM/MAML paradigm (Lee & Khairoutdinov, 2015; Lin et al., 2023).

### 4.1 Two-Leaf C₃/C₄ Optimality Acclimation, Leaf Dark Respiration, and Stomatal Closure
- **Primary References**: Farquhar, von Caemmerer, & Berry (1980); de Pury & Farquhar (1997); Bernacchi et al. (2001, 2003); Dai, Dickinson, & Wang (2004); Medlyn et al. (2011); Prentice et al. (2014); Heskel et al. (2016); Wang et al. (2017); Stocker et al. (2020); Mengoli et al. (2022); Scott & Smith (2022); Luo et al. (2024); Ren et al. (2024, 2025); Lavergne et al. (2026).
- **Midday Downward-SW EMA & Closed-Form Two-Leaf Capacity Allocation**:
  1. Midday ($11\text{:}30\text{--}12\text{:}30$ local solar time) incident downward visible direct-beam and diffuse shortwave fluxes $(\mathrm{VIS}_b, \mathrm{VIS}_d)$, midday solar-zenith cosine $\cos\theta_z$, daytime temperature, VPD, and nighttime temperature are smoothed daily via a 14-day exponential moving average (EMA).
  2. Applying spherical-leaf two-stream canopy radiative transfer (de Pury & Farquhar, 1997) to the EMA-smoothed midday downward irradiances $(\widetilde{\mathrm{VIS}}_b, \widetilde{\mathrm{VIS}}_d, \widetilde{\mu}_0)$ at the current snow-exposed canopy leaf area index $L_c$ determines the acclimation absorbed PPFD profile $\tilde{I}_l(L) = \tilde{I}_{lb}(L) + \tilde{I}_{ld}(L)$ and whole-canopy $\tilde{I}_c$, driving PFT-free EEO big-leaf capacity acclimation for C₃ ($V_{c\max 25}, J_{\max 25}$) and C₄ ($V_{c\max 25}, J_{\max 25}, V_{p\max 25}$) together with coupled leaf dark respiration $R_{25}$ (Heskel et al., 2016; Ren et al., 2024, 2025).
  3. At each sub-daily timestep, whole-canopy capacities $X \in \{V_{c\max 25}, J_{\max 25}, V_{p\max 25}\}$ distribute vertically as $X_l(L) = X[\tilde{I}_{lb}(L) + \tilde{I}_{ld}(L)]/\tilde{I}_c$ and integrate analytically over the instantaneous sunlit fraction $f_{\mathrm{sun}}(L) = \exp(-k_{db}L)$ to yield closed-form sunlit and shaded capacities $X_{\mathrm{sun}}$ and $X_{\mathrm{sh}} = \max(10^{-6}, X - X_{\mathrm{sun}})$ (de Pury & Farquhar, 1997), solving sunlit and shaded leaf temperatures, optimal stomatal conductances, and C₃/C₄ dominance $F_4 \in [0, 1]$ (Dai et al., 2004; Luo et al., 2024; Lavergne et al., 2026).

### 4.2 Evapotranspiration Partitioning, Map-Free Albedo, Root-Zone Sizing, Multi-Layer Soil, and Snow
- **Primary References**: Clapp & Hornberger (1978); Sellers et al. (1986); Yang et al. (1997); Takata et al. (2003); Dai et al. (2004, App. A); Sakaguchi & Zeng (2009); Swenson & Lawrence (2012); Oleson et al. (2013); Gao et al. (2014); Nitta et al. (2014); Lee & Khairoutdinov (2015); Davis et al. (2017); MATSIRO6 Document Writing Team (2021); Cai et al. (2025); Nishizawa et al. (2025, Sec. 8.7.1); Zhou et al. (2025).
- **Three-Component ET & Prognostic EEO LAI**: Total land latent heat flux sums sunlit/shaded transpiration (bounded by drought-bridging root-zone capacity $S_R$ and supply rate $S_w$; Gao et al., 2014; Davis et al., 2017), water-limited wet-canopy interception evaporation ($f_{\mathrm{wet}} = [W_c/(p_{\mathrm{liq}} L)]^{2/3}$, $p_{\mathrm{liq}} = 0.1\ \mathrm{kg\ m^{-2}}$; CLM5 Tech. Note, Ch. 5 & 7), and bare-soil/under-canopy evaporation regulated by the CLM5 dry-surface-layer soil resistance and under-canopy stability (Swenson & Lawrence, 2014; Sakaguchi & Zeng, 2009). Prognostic $\mathrm{LAI}$ tracks the EEO Lambert-$W_0$ steady state (Cai et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2025).
- **Map-Free Two-Band Surface Albedo & Cryosphere**: Bare-soil VIS/NIR albedos vary continuously with topsoil saturation (Oleson et al., 2013; Lee & Khairoutdinov, 2015) beneath singularity-free two-stream canopy reflectance (Dai et al., 2004, App. A). Snow cover incorporates subgrid topographic/hysteretic fractional area $A_{\mathrm{sn}}$ (Swenson & Lawrence, 2012; Nitta et al., 2014), snow-burial masking of canopy height and $\mathrm{LAI}$ (MATSIRO6, 2021, Eqs. 2.1–2.3), prognostic temperature-aging VIS/NIR snow albedo with solar-zenith dependence (Yang et al., 1997; MATSIRO6, 2021, Sec. 8.8), and glacier runoff capping ($S_{n,\max} = 1000\ \mathrm{kg\ m^{-2}}$; MATSIRO6, 2021, Eq. 8.44), with all land liquid/ice runoff returned conservatively to the ocean to close global water and fusion-heat budgets (MIROC6, 2021, Sec. 4.1.2).
- **Multi-Layer Freeze–Thaw Soil Thermodynamics**: Five-layer implicit Thomas heat conduction down to $\sim 3\ \mathrm{m}$ depth with moisture-dependent thermal conductivity and instantaneous $0\ ^\circ\mathrm{C}$ liquid–ice phase change resolves diurnal/seasonal continental thermal inertia and permafrost zero-curtain dynamics (MATSIRO6, 2021, Ch. 10; Nishizawa et al., 2025, Sec. 8.7.1).

### 4.3 1.5-Layer Wind-Driven Voronoi Ekman Slab Ocean and Fractional-Lead Sea Ice
- **Primary References**: Semtner (1976); Briegleb et al. (1986); Miller, Beljaars, & Palmer (1992); Codron (2012); MIROC6 AGCM Document Writing Team (2021, Sec. 3.8); Nishizawa et al. (2025, Sec. 8.6).
- **Zero-$Q$-Flux Voronoi Ekman + Shallow-Water Ocean & Sea Ice**: Discretized on the host SCVT Voronoi C-grid (`normal velocity & transport = 0` at coastlines), wind stress drives both mixed-layer ($H_{\mathrm{ml}} = 50\ \mathrm{m}$) Ekman heat transport and 1.5-layer reduced-gravity shallow-water dynamics ($g' = 0.03\ \mathrm{m\ s^{-2}}$, TRiSK Coriolis, Munk eddy viscosity, Sverdrup/western-boundary gyres, and equatorial Kelvin/Rossby waves) with **zero prescribed $Q$-flux** (Codron, 2012), coupled to thermodynamic sea ice (Semtner, 1976) with fractional lead coverage $f_i = \min(\sqrt{M_i/M_c}, 1)$, area-weighted open-water/ice fluxes, snowfall fusion cooling, and solar-zenith-dependent ocean direct/diffuse albedo (Briegleb et al., 1986; MIROC6, 2021, Sec. 3.8; Nishizawa et al., 2025, Sec. 8.6).

## 5. Dynamic / Accelerated SIA Ice-Sheet Model and Unified TOML Parameter Architecture

### 5.1 Dynamic Equilibrium SIA Ice Sheet and Barometric Vertical Remapping (`src/aoba/icesheet.py`)
- **Primary References**: Vialov (1958); Mahaffy (1976); Le Meur & Huybrechts (1996); Bueler et al. (2005); Cuffey & Paterson (2010); Pollard & DeConto (2012).
- **Coupling Architecture**: Initializes bedrock and ice thickness from `Earth2014.BED2014` and `ICE2014`, drives Glen's flow law ($n = 3$) upstream finite-volume SIA diffusion on the SCVT mesh using EMA-filtered annual SMB and relaxed lithospheric isostasy under configurable spinup acceleration $\alpha_{\mathrm{eff}}(t)$, updates Klemp STF vertical levels conservatively with barometric below-surface extrapolation and log-pressure remapping across host and CRM columns, and handles dynamic `SURF_LAND <-> SURF_ICESHEET` mask transitions with pioneer vegetation seeding upon deglaciation.

### 5.2 Single-File TOML Registry & Interactive Configurator (`src/aoba/params.py`, `scripts/aoba_config_gui.py`)
- **Model Transparency**: Centralizes all grid resolutions (`glevel`, `nz`, CRM subgrid dimensions), orbital/planetary forcings, dynamic ice-sheet controls, output streams/cadences (mean vs instantaneous, 2D/3D/subgrid 2D), and physical constants (13 sections, 204 parameters) in [`config/aoba_default.toml`](../config/aoba_default.toml), paired with a zero-dependency interactive Web GUI (`scripts/aoba_config_gui.py`) featuring live SCVT/CRM/vertical visualizations and direct execution via `scripts/run_g5_30yr.py --config ...`.
