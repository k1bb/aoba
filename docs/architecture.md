# AOBA Model Architecture and Literature-Grounded Formulation

> **Language**: English | [日本語 (architecture_ja.md)](architecture_ja.md)

## 1. Scientific Objective

AOBA investigates two foundational Earth-system questions:
1. Given **only** minimal external physical boundary conditions — land–sea/ice-sheet geometry and topography/bathymetry from `Earth2014` (Hirt & Rexer, 2015), planetary rotation rate $\Omega$, orbital solar insolation $S_0$, and atmospheric greenhouse-gas concentrations — can a coupled atmosphere–ocean–cryosphere–biosphere–chemistry Earth System Model autonomously reproduce Earth's climate zones, ocean circulation, clouds, aerosols, and ecosystems (benchmarked against Beck et al., 2018, 2023)?
2. How does such a minimally constrained ESM — free of cumulus parameterizations, prescribed ocean $Q$-fluxes, soil/albedo lookup maps, plant-functional-type (PFT) tables, and pre-trained climate ML weights — respond to abrupt radiative forcing ($4\times\mathrm{CO}_2$), paleoclimate orbital shifts, and altered planetary parameters?

## 2. Unified Nonhydrostatic Dynamics, Turbulence, Gravity Waves, and Aerodynamic Datum

### 2.1 Global Host Core: Spherical Centroidal Voronoi C-Grid (TRiSK) + Charney–Phillips HEVI (`src/aoba/scvt_dynamics.py`)
- **Primary References**: Ringler, Ju, & Gunzburger (2008, *Ocean Dynamics*, 58, 475–498); Thuburn, Ringler, Skamarock, & Klemp (2009, *J. Comput. Phys.*, 228, 8321–8335); Ringler, Thuburn, Klemp, & Skamarock (2010, *J. Comput. Phys.*, 229, 3065–3090); Klemp (2011, *Mon. Wea. Rev.*, 139, 2163–2169); Staniforth & Thuburn (2012, *Q. J. R. Meteorol. Soc.*, 138, 1–26); Skamarock et al. (2012, *Mon. Wea. Rev.*, 140, 3090–3105); Gassmann (2013, *Q. J. R. Meteorol. Soc.*, 139, 152–175); Zalesak (1979, *J. Comput. Phys.*, 31, 335–362); Held & Suarez (1994); Nishizawa, Tomita, & Team SCALE (2025, *SCALE-RM v5.5.5*, Ch. 2).
- **Governing Equations**: Three-dimensional flux-form compressible nonhydrostatic Euler equations conserving dry-air density $\rho_d$, edge-normal momentum, vertical momentum $\rho_d w$, moist potential temperature $\rho_d \theta_m$ (with virtual vapor-buoyancy and compressible diabatic enthalpy terms; Seidel & Yang, 2024; Nishizawa et al., 2025), and moist/tracer scalars transported via positive-definite Zalesak Flux-Corrected Transport (FCT).
- **SCVT Orthogonal C-Grid & Vertical Coordinate**: Recursive icosahedral subdivision with Lloyd optimization supports `Glevel-4` (2,562 cells, $\sim 448\ \mathrm{km}$), `Glevel-5` (10,242 cells, $\sim 224\ \mathrm{km}$), `Glevel-6` (40,962 cells, $\sim 112\ \mathrm{km}$), and `Glevel-7` (163,842 cells, $\sim 56\ \mathrm{km}$), with resolution-scaled Smagorinsky mixing and biharmonic $\nabla^4$ hyperviscosity. Vertical Charney–Phillips staggering over Klemp (2011) height-smoothed terrain scales cleanly from `nz = 32` ($z_{\mathrm{top}} = 42\ \mathrm{km}$) to high-top stratosphere-resolving `nz = 64` ($z_{\mathrm{top}} = 65\ \mathrm{km}$, $\Delta z_1 = 60\ \mathrm{m}$).
- **Orographic & Map-Free Non-Orographic Gravity-Wave Drag (`src/aoba/orography.py`, `src/aoba/gravity_wave.py`)**: Combines subgrid low-level mountain blocking and tropospheric orographic GWD derived directly from 5-arc-min `Earth2014` relief (Lott & Miller, 1997) with map-free flow-dependent frontal and convective non-orographic GWD spectra (Charron & Manzini, 2002; Beres, 2004; Richter et al., 2010).

### 2.2 Embedded 2D/3D CRM, MYNN Level 2.5 PBL, and 3D SGS Turbulence (`src/aoba/crm.py`, `src/aoba/turbulence.py`)
- **Primary References**: Khairoutdinov & Randall (2003); Khairoutdinov, Randall, & DeMott (2005); Lee & Khairoutdinov (2015); Lin et al. (2023); Nakanishi & Niino (2009, *JMSJ*, 87, 895–912); Yamazaki & Miura (2024); Nishizawa et al. (2025, Sec. 8.1.2 & 8.2.1).
- **Identical Parent–Child Nonhydrostatic Core**: Each host Voronoi cell embeds a periodic compressible C-grid/Charney–Phillips RK3-HEVI CRM (configurable from 2D curtains such as `32 × 1` at $\Delta x = 2\ \mathrm{km}$ to 3D `8 × 8` or `64 × 16` `RCE_large` domains) sharing the identical thermodynamics, radiation, microphysics, and turbulence solvers with the global host, coupled to implicit **MYNN Level 2.5** vertical PBL mixing and anisotropic **Smagorinsky–Lilly** 3D SGS diffusion.

### 2.3 Displacement-Following Aerodynamic Datum, RSL Similarity, & Implicit Surface Coupling (`src/aoba/surface.py`)
- **Primary References**: Hirt & Rexer (2015); Kempes et al. (2011); Raupach (1994); Harman & Finnigan (2007); De Ridder (2010); Bonan et al. (2018); Lee et al. (2020); Abolafia-Rosenzweig et al. (2021); Meier et al. (2022); Miller, Beljaars, & Palmer (1992); Kalnay & Kanamitsu (1988).
- **Canopy Datum & Conductance Stability**: Prognostic canopy height $h_c$ (adjusted for snow burial) determines continuous zero-plane displacement $d$, roughness length $z_{0m}$, and analytical exponential-integral Roughness Sublayer (RSL) corrections $\hat{\Psi}_{M,H}$ above $\tilde{z}_{\mathrm{ref}} = \max(z_{c,0} - z_{w,0} - 0.65 h_c,\ 5.0\ \mathrm{m})$. A $C^\infty$-smooth quadratic Kalnay–Kanamitsu implicit surface–atmosphere conductance limiter ($\alpha_{\max} = 1.50$) suppresses $2\Delta t$ sensible-heat oscillations over tall forest canopies on thin lowest atmospheric layers ($\Delta z_1 = 60\ \mathrm{m}$) without breaking daytime flux sensitivity.

## 3. Online Surrogate Superparameterization (`OSSP`: Zero-Pretraining ML Acceleration; `src/aoba/crm_self_distill.py`)

- **Primary References**: Beucler et al. (2021, *Phys. Rev. Lett.*, 126, 098302); Patton, Sullivan, & Moeng (2005, *J. Atmos. Sci.*, 62, 2078–2097); Mapes & Neale (2011, *JAMES*, 3, M06004); Colin et al. (2019, *J. Atmos. Sci.*, 76, 1353–1376); Yu et al. (2023/2025, *NeurIPS* / *JMLR*, ClimSim); Kochkov et al. (2024, *Nature*, 632, 1060–1066).
- **Online Teacher–Student Distillation**: Setting `enable_crm_self_distill = true` and `enable_rad_self_distill = true` runs AOBA's genuine nonhydrostatic CRM and full ICA/3D-TICA radiative transfer strictly on a rotating, spatially stratified + high-epistemic-uncertainty teacher subset (`1 / crm_distill_stride` columns, preserving convective memory via continuous dwell windows and a 50% persistent anchor cohort), while two-head epistemic residual MLPs (`CRMSelfDistillNet` and `RadSelfDistillNet`) advance the remaining student columns via dense GPU GEMMs and train continuously online from GPU-resident dual-zone replay buffers (recency ring + long-horizon reservoir).
- **Map-Free Features, Organization Invariants, & Exact Conservation Closures**: Inputs consist exclusively of local conserved moist thermodynamic profiles $(T_{li}, \theta_l, q_n, q_n/q_{\mathrm{sat}})$, three prognostic convective-memory scalars $(M_{P,\mathrm{fast}}, M_{P,\mathrm{slow}}, M_{B,\mathrm{slow}})$, and 16 periodic 2D/3D surface-heterogeneity clustering descriptors (Moran's $I$, patch length scale $L_{\mathrm{patch}}$, $\rho(\mathrm{SHF}, \mathrm{LHF})$). Fused GPU kernels (`crm_distill_closure_col`, `rad_distill_closure_col`) enforce exact algebraic conservation of column water mass, sensible/latent enthalpy, horizontal momentum, night-side zero shortwave flux, and per-subcolumn TOA–atmosphere–surface radiative energy closure, paired with a periodic `SubgridSpatialHead` reconstructing zero-mean cold-pool microclimate and gust outflow onto MAML surface tiles.
- **Teacher-Only `SparseCRMPool` & Prequential Shadow Diagnostics**: For large 3D CRMs with decoupled surface tiles (`crm_sfc_tiles > 0`), `SparseCRMPool` allocates full 3D CRM state solely on active teacher slots (`~0.15 GB` vs. `~37 GB` for G5 `64 × 16`). Enabling `enable_distill_shadow_diag = true` evaluates both surrogates on active teacher columns strictly before each gradient update, reporting unbiased out-of-sample host-atmosphere and land-tile RMSEs and precipitation anomaly correlations in real time across climate transitions.

## 4. Selectable Radiation, Cloud Microphysics, and Non-Inductive Lightning

### 4.1 Radiative Transfer (`rad_scheme` + 3D-TICA; `src/aoba/radiation*.py`)
- **Primary References**: Toon et al. (1989); Lacis & Hansen (1974); Slingo (1989); Ebert & Curry (1992); Hu & Stamnes (1993); Cahalan et al. (1994); Chou et al. (1999); Várnai & Davies (1999); Sekiguchi & Nakajima (2008); Pincus et al. (2019, *JAMES*, 11, 3074–3089); Mlawer et al. (2023).
- **Unified Selectable Schemes**: Four schemes share one two-stream tridiagonal interface:
  1. `"four_band"`: Fast analytical 2 SW + 2 LW solver.
  2. `"ckd"`: Deterministic 16-band (50 $g$-point) Correlated-$k$ solver ($\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}, \mathrm{CO}_2, \mathrm{O}_3, \mathrm{CH}_4, \mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}$).
  3. `"ckd_hd"`: 111 $g$-point high-detail CKD with Chou LW cloud scattering and 5 aerosol species.
  4. `"rrtmgp"`: Official 240 $g$-point (128 LW + 112 SW) HITRAN / MT\_CKD 4.1 $k$-distribution solver with optional 3D Tilted Independent Column Approximation (`enable_rad_3d_tica = true`) capturing cloud-side illumination and shadowing.

### 4.2 Cloud Microphysics & Selectable Lightning (`microphysics_scheme`, `lightning_scheme`, `src/aoba/microphysics*.py`, `src/aoba/lightning*.py`)
- **Primary References**: Khairoutdinov & Randall (2003); Khairoutdinov & Kogan (2000); Morrison et al. (2005); Seifert & Beheng (2006); Morrison & Gettelman (2008); DeMott et al. (2010); Boutle et al. (2014); McCaul et al. (2009); Price & Rind (1993); Schumann & Huntrieser (2007); Takahashi (1978); Chiu (1978); Dwyer (2003); Fierro et al. (2013); Sato et al. (2019).
- **Selectable Schemes**: `"one_moment"` (2-variable diagnostic liquid/ice), `"two_moment"` (prognostic cloud liquid/ice number concentrations $n_c, n_i$ with natural sea-salt/sulfate/dust CCN & INP activation and resolution-aware KK00 LWC-variance scaling), and `"six_class_2m"` (full 6-class 2-moment: vapor, cloud liquid, cloud ice, rain, snow, graupel). Lightning (`lightning_scheme`) offers both the fast `"diagnostic"` non-inductive ice–graupel charging proxy (`src/aoba/lightning.py`) and the optional SCALE-LT-class `"explicit_bulk"` 5-species hydrometeor electrification, exact FFT + Thomas Poisson electric-field, and dielectric-breakdown IC/CG discharge scheme (`src/aoba/lightning_explicit.py`), supplying mass-conserving $\mathrm{LNO}_x$ emissions to ozone chemistry and land/ocean nitrogen budgets.

## 5. Autonomous EEO Biosphere, MAML Land Surface, Lakes, C/N Permafrost, Wildfire, and Rivers

### 5.1 Two-Leaf $\mathrm{C}_3/\mathrm{C}_4$ Optimality Biosphere (`src/aoba/biosphere.py`)
- **Primary References**: Farquhar, von Caemmerer, & Berry (1980); de Pury & Farquhar (1997); Bernacchi et al. (2001, 2003); Dai, Dickinson, & Wang (2004); Medlyn et al. (2011); Prentice et al. (2014); Heskel et al. (2016); Smith et al. (2019); Kumarathunge et al. (2019); Stocker et al. (2020); Mengoli et al. (2022); Scott & Smith (2022); Kikuzawa (1991); Givnish (2002); Sakai & Larcher (1987); Wang et al. (2023); Luo et al. (2024); Ren et al. (2024, 2025); Schneider, Gessler, & Stocker (2025); Cai et al. (2025); Zhou et al. (2025); Gan et al. (2026); Lavergne et al. (2026).
- **Formulation**: Combines midday-SW EMA two-stream canopy light acclimation, three-pillar thermal acclimation ($T_{\mathrm{growth}}, T_{\mathrm{home}}$), closed-form sunlit/shaded two-leaf partitioning, discrete-crown clumping and optimal leaf angle $\chi_L$, time-integrated soil-moisture optimality $\tau(D_{\max})$ (Lu et al., 2016; Holtzman et al., 2024), drought-bridging root-zone capacity $S_R$ (Gao et al., 2014; Davis et al., 2017), autonomous evergreen/deciduous optimality $f_{\mathrm{evg}}$ with winter frost mortality, Lambert-$W_0$ prognostic LAI, and vascular canopy height $h_c$.

### 5.2 MAML Subgrid Orography, Wetlands, Dynamic Lakes, Soil/Snow, C/N Permafrost, Wildfire, & Rivers
- **Primary References**: Clapp & Hornberger (1978); Beven & Kirkby (1979); Yang et al. (1997); Sakaguchi & Zeng (2009); Flanner et al. (2007); Mironov (2008); Thonicke et al. (2010); Swenson & Lawrence (2012, 2014); Koven et al. (2013); Oleson et al. (2013); Barnes et al. (2014); Fiddes & Gruber (2014); Nitta et al. (2014); Mizukami et al. (2016); MATSIRO6 (2021); Dong et al. (2022); Stocker & Prentice (2024); Regnier et al. (2013).
- **Integrated Land System**:
  - **MAML Subgrid Terrain & Wetlands (`src/aoba/maml_land_subgrid.py`)**: Elevation-band microclimate downscaling (consistent barometric scaling of both surface and lowest-level pressures) and TOPMODEL subgrid wetland fraction.
  - **Map-Free Dynamic Lakes (`src/aoba/lakes.py`)**: Prognoses depression water volume on `Earth2014` Priority-Flood basins coupled to 2-layer FLake thermodynamics and lake-ice freezing/melting.
  - **Soil, Snow & Cryosphere (`src/aoba/surface.py`)**: 5-layer freeze–thaw implicit soil heat conduction and Richards hydrology, soil-moisture-dependent bare albedo, aging snowpack with BC/dust SNICAR darkening, and glacier capping.
  - **Terrestrial C/N & MAML SPITFIRE Wildfire (`src/aoba/land_carbon.py`)**: Coupled leaf/wood/root/litter + 5-layer permafrost C/N pools with Neumaier–Kahan summation, biological N fixation, leaching, $\phi_N$ limitation feedback, and discrete subcolumn SPITFIRE wildfire with 2D neighbor spread and pyrogenic BC/OC/C emissions.
  - **Priority-Flood River & Solute Routing (`src/aoba/river_routing.py`)**: Map-free Voronoi drainage network routing freshwater, alkalinity, dissolved organic/inorganic nutrients, and bioavailable iron conservatively to coastal ocean cells.

## 6. Selectable Ocean (`ocean_mode`), EVP-ITD Sea Ice, Waves, FlexPFT BGC, & Offline Deep-Ocean Acceleration

- **Primary References**: Semtner (1976); Briegleb et al. (1986); Redi (1982); Gent & McWilliams (1990); Large et al. (1994); Hunke & Dukowicz (1997); Griffies & Hallberg (2000); Adcroft & Campin (2004); Shchepetkin & McWilliams (2005); Fox-Kemper et al. (2008); Codron (2012); Hallberg (2013); Roquet et al. (2015); Orr & Epitalon (2015); Danilov et al. (2017); Orr et al. (2017); Turner et al. (2022); Sasai et al. (2022); Takaya et al. (2010); Andreas et al. (2015).
- **3D $z^*$ FCT SCVT Voronoi OGCM (`src/aoba/ocean_ogcm.py`, `src/aoba/ocean_coupler.py`)**: Supports independent fine ocean meshes (`ocean_glevel = 6–7`, `ocean_nz = 18–64` from 2 m top layer to 6,000 m), exact $z^*$ vertical coordinate, Zalesak FCT tracer advection, Roquet (2015) EOS, Hallberg scale-aware GM90 + rotated Redi tensor, Fox–Kemper submesoscale restratification, KPP + Ri shear + internal-tide deep mixing, current-relative wind stress, resolution-aware C-grid Hollingsworth–Bernoulli divergence damping ($a_{\mathrm{div}} = 0.030\,\Delta c_{\min}^2/\Delta t$), and exact total water+ice enthalpy surface coupling. Lightweight 1.5-layer Ekman–shallow-water mode (`ocean_mode = "ekman_sw"`, `src/aoba/ocean_ekman.py`) remains available for rapid idealized experiments.
- **EVP-ITD Sea Ice, Surface Waves, & FlexPFT Marine BGC (`src/aoba/seaice_evp.py`, `src/aoba/ocean_waves.py`, `src/aoba/ocean_bgc.py`)**: 5-category Voronoi EVP-ITD sea ice with ridging and drift, prognostic wind-wave roughness/Stokes-drift/Langmuir-turbulence coupling, and FlexPFT marine biogeochemistry with OMIP/mocsy 2.0 carbonate chemistry, $\mathrm{O}_2$/OMZ denitrification, and atmospheric dust-Fe / $\mathrm{LNO}_x$ / riverine solute coupling.
- **Phase-2 Offline Deep-Ocean Acceleration (`scripts/run_offline_ogcm_spinup.py`)**: Drives the standalone 3D OGCM + FlexPFT BGC + EVP-ITD sea-ice core with multi-year Phase-1 atmospheric fluxes (auto-stopping at `< 0.01 K/century` deep drift) to reach millennial deep-ocean equilibrium at a fraction of coupled compute cost.

## 7. Atmospheric Chemistry, Aerosols, Water Isotopes, Dynamic Ice Sheets, and Planetary Extensions

- **Prognostic 3D Ozone (`src/aoba/ozone_chemistry.py`, `src/aoba/ozone_forcing.py`)**: Selectable `o3_mode` (`rcemip`, `latitude_seasonal`, `climatology`, `prognostic` 3D Chapman + $\mathrm{HO}_x/\mathrm{NO}_x/\mathrm{ClO}_x$/PSC photochemistry + Wesely dry deposition + Brewer–Dobson FCT transport; Solomon et al., 1996; Brasseur & Solomon, 2005; Burkholder et al., 2019).
- **Natural Aerosols, Dust, & Water Isotopes (`src/aoba/aerosols.py`, `src/aoba/dust.py`, `src/aoba/water_isotopes.py`)**: Map-free 4-species aerosols (sea salt, FlexPFT DMS/$\mathrm{SO}_4$, wildfire BC/OC, BVOC-SOA; Gong, 2003; Jaeglé et al., 2011; Guenther et al., 2012), saltation mineral dust (Marticorena & Bergametti, 1995; Zender et al., 2003), direct radiative forcing and snow darkening, plus 3D stable water isotopes ($\mathrm{H}_2^{18}\mathrm{O}$, $\mathrm{HDO}$; Craig & Gordon, 1965; Majoube, 1971; Merlivat & Jouzel, 1979).
- **Optional Carbon/Trace-Gas Cycles, Volcanic/Solar, Dynamic SIA Ice Sheet, & Exoplanets (`src/aoba/prognostic_co2.py`, `src/aoba/trace_gas_cycle.py`, `src/aoba/volcanic_solar.py`, `src/aoba/planetary_geochem.py`, `src/aoba/icesheet.py`, `src/aoba/planetary_presets.py`)**: Supports `co2_mode = constant / scenario / csv / prognostic`, prognostic $\mathrm{CH}_4/\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}$, volcanic stratospheric $\mathrm{SO}_2$ and solar cycle forcing, silicate weathering & Laskar (2004) Milankovitch orbital evolution, Glen-law ($n=3$) accelerated SIA ice sheets with lithospheric isostasy and barometric vertical remapping (Bueler et al., 2005; Pollard & DeConto, 2012), and rocky exoplanet presets (`proxima_b`, `trappist_1e`, `mars_early`).

## 8. Unified 323-Parameter Registry, 104-Kernel Metal/CUDA Backend, & Diagnostics

- **Centralized TOML & `AOBA Studio` (`src/aoba/params.py`, `src/aoba/param_docs.py`, `scripts/aoba_config_gui.py`)**: Consolidates all **323 parameters across 14 sections** in [`config/aoba_default.toml`](../config/aoba_default.toml) with 100% bilingual physical documentation, **11 experiment presets** in `config/presets/`, universal cross-grid restart remapping (`AG4 → AG6`, arbitrary `nz` and `ocean_glevel`), 30-class Köppen–Geiger classification (`src/aoba/koppen.py`), and CMIP7 Data Request v1.2 Zarr v3 output (`src/aoba/cmip7_diag.py`, `src/aoba/io_zarr.py`).
- **104-Kernel Single-Source Metal/CUDA + CUDA Graphs Backend (`src/aoba/kernels*.py`, `src/aoba/kernels_cuda.py`, `src/aoba/runtime.py`)**: All **104 fused GPU kernels** (covering SCVT RK3-HEVI dynamics, 2D/3D CRM, FCT transport, 1M/2M/6-class microphysics, RRTMGP/CKD-HD/CKD/4-band radiation, 3D prognostic ozone, MYNN PBL, compacted surface/biosphere Newton–Raphson solvers, MAML/lakes/C-N/fire/diagnostic & explicit-bulk lightning, dust/aerosols/isotopes, 5-category EVP-ITD sea ice, 3D $z^*$ OGCM/KPP/GM/Redi/FlexPFT BGC, OGW/NGWD, and OSSP ML feature/closure/spatial-head/ensemble kernels) are authored once in Metal Shading Language and deterministically transpiled to native CUDA C++17 on NVIDIA GPUs (`cuda` → `mps` → `cpu`), paired with automatic CUDA Graph capture/replay and persistent cloud/HPC monthly checkpoints.
